## What are Slackbot Skills?

Instead of Slackbot giving you a generic, one-off answer every time, Skills let you encode repeatable processes. Think of them as executable Standard Operating Procedures that live inside your workspace. You describe the steps, Slackbot follows them. No more re-explaining how to run a client kickoff or triage a support ticket. The process becomes the process.

Slackbot automatically finds and uses the right Skill when you ask a relevant question. You can also manually attach a Skill to a prompt the same way you attach a file, channel, or person.

## What makes Skills a game-changer

- **Anyone on the team can build one** — No admin or developer needed. Slackbot can help you build it by asking a few questions, or you can create one from scratch with your own steps and output format.
- **They're shareable** — Push Skills to the channels or people who need them. Institutional knowledge stops living in one person's head and starts living in the workspace itself.
- **Assignable by role** — Owners and admins can hand the right Skills to the right people based on their job requirements. Onboarding a process becomes a five-minute setup.
- **Import from any Canvas** — If you've documented a process in a Slack Canvas, you're one click away from making it executable.

## How to create your first Skill

Open Slack, click Slackbot at the top of your screen, and navigate to the Skills tab. You have two options:

1. **Create with Slackbot** — Slackbot walks you through questions, drafts the Skill, and asks for your approval.
2. **Create from scratch** — Write the description, name, step-by-step instructions, and expected output format yourself.

Once created, share it with your team and Slackbot will start using it automatically. You can edit Skills anytime — manually or by asking Slackbot to make changes.
