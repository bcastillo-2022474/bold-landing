Marketing and ecommerce teams waste hours switching between GA4, Ads Manager, and Shopify dashboards. Go Insights brings everything into Slack with intelligent alerts that surface what actually matters.

What is Google Analytics Insights? It is a Slack app that connects GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Search Console, Plausible, and Shopify directly to your Slack workspace. It monitors your marketing performance 24/7 and sends intelligent alerts when metrics change — spend spikes, conversion drops, SEO shifts, or tracking failures.

Adaptive baselines learn each account's normal behavior automatically. Every alert includes full context: what changed, how significant it is, and where to investigate. No manual thresholds to maintain.

Key features include ad spend monitoring across Google Ads and Meta Ads with root cause analysis, conversion and revenue anomaly detection, SEO tracking via Google Search Console, Shopify ecommerce performance monitoring, automated weekly digests for stakeholders, and agency-ready multi-account routing to different Slack channels.

For marketing teams, Go Insights removes dashboard fatigue. Your team stops refreshing Ads Manager looking for problems. Alerts arrive with enough context to act immediately. Sensitivity adapts to each account automatically.

For ecommerce teams, Go Insights is a revenue safety net. Detect revenue anomalies before they compound. Catch tracking failures before they corrupt your data. Know when campaigns stop delivering before ROAS suffers. See GA4, Shopify, Google Ads, and Meta Ads in one Slack channel.

Installation takes two clicks through the Slack Marketplace via standard OAuth. No admin configuration, no custom apps, no webhooks. Data is processed in memory and destroyed immediately after delivery — no sensitive analytics data is permanently stored. A free preview is available with your real historical data, no credit card required.
