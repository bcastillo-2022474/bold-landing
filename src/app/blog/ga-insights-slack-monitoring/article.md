## What is Google Analytics Insights?

Google Analytics Insights (Go Insights) is a Slack app that connects GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Search Console, Plausible, and Shopify directly to your Slack workspace. It monitors your marketing performance 24/7 and sends intelligent alerts when metrics change — spend spikes, conversion drops, SEO shifts, or tracking failures.

Adaptive baselines learn each account's normal behavior automatically. No manual thresholds to set. No noisy false alarms. Every alert includes context: what changed, how significant, where to investigate.

## Key Features

- **Ad spend monitoring** — Catch pacing issues and overspend across Google Ads and Meta Ads with root cause analysis (e.g., CPM increases without CTR improvement)
- **Conversion and revenue alerts** — Detect mobile conversion drops, checkout regressions, and sudden revenue anomalies
- **SEO and traffic monitoring** — Google Search Console integration tracks visibility changes and position shifts
- **Shopify ecommerce performance** — Monitor store revenue, orders, and product performance directly in Slack
- **Weekly digests** — Automated summaries of wins, issues, and performance shifts; perfect for stakeholders
- **Agency support** — Route different accounts to different Slack channels for multi-client management
