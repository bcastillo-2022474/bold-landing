Overview

Slack helps you centralize work communications, including emails. There are three ways to send emails to Slack: dedicated channel email addresses (paid plans), Gmail and Outlook plugins (all plans), and Slackbot forwarding (all plans).

---

Method 1: Channel Email Address (Paid Plans)

Create a unique email address for any channel or DM. Anyone with the address can send emails directly into that conversation.

Steps:
1. Open the channel or DM.
2. Click the name in the conversation header.
3. Click the Integrations tab.
4. Select "Send emails to this channel" or "Send emails to this conversation".
5. Click "Get email address" to generate a unique address.
6. Copy the address and set up auto-forwarding in your email client.

Customize how emails appear: Click "Customize email appearance" to set a custom icon and display name.

Remove an email address: Open the conversation, go to Integrations, select the email option, and click "Delete address".

---

Method 2: Gmail and Outlook Plugins (All Plans)

Slack for Gmail:
1. Install from Google Workspace Marketplace.
2. Open an email and click the Slack icon in the right sidebar.
3. Connect to Slack and authorize access.
4. Choose a channel or person and click "Send to [name]".

Slack for Outlook:
1. Install from Microsoft AppSource (Office 365 only).
2. Open an email and click the Slack icon (desktop) or the three dots menu (browser).
3. Select a channel or person and click "Send to Slack".

Note: Not compatible with on-premises Exchange, POP/IMAP, or national cloud deployments.

---

Method 3: Slackbot Forwarding (All Plans)

1. Click your profile picture in the sidebar.
2. Go to Preferences > Messages & Media.
3. Scroll to "Bring your emails into Slack" and click "Get a forwarding address".
4. Copy the address and configure a forwarding rule in your email client.

To disable: Go back to Preferences and click "Disable this address".

---

Method Comparison

Channel Email Address | Paid Plans | Team-wide visibility, auto-forwarding
Gmail/Outlook Plugin | All Plans | Individual email forwarding
Slackbot Forwarding | All Plans | Personal inbox consolidation

FAQ

Q: Can I send emails to Slack on a free plan?
A: Yes. Plugins and Slackbot forwarding work on all plans. Channel email addresses require a paid plan.

Q: Does HIPAA compliance affect this?
A: Yes. HIPAA-compliant Enterprise Grid organizations cannot send emails to Slack.

Q: Why are my emails not showing up?
A: Incoming emails may be disabled for your workspace. Contact an owner or admin.

Q: Can I customize how forwarded emails look?
A: Yes for channel addresses (custom icon and name). Plugins and Slackbot use default formatting.
