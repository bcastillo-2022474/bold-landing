FROM A GENERAL AI TO YOUR COMPANY'S AI BRAIN

A general-purpose AI understands CRMs and tickets in the abstract. It does not know that your company calls a workflow Pipeline Rescue, that Slack channels map to customers, or that a specific approval is required before a deal moves. Fine-tuning teaches domain behavior: terminology, request structure, classification, sales handling, tool calling, workflows, and human approval.

WHY OPEN-WEIGHT MODELS CHANGE THE EQUATION

Closed models limit customization, infrastructure, data control, and cost. Muse Glimmer is a 30-billion-parameter open-weight agentic model that can be downloaded, customized, and deployed — including locally on consumer hardware — with training that combines supervised fine-tuning, on-policy distillation, and reinforcement learning.

FINE-TUNING + SLACK = AN AI OPERATING LAYER

Architecture: Slack → AI Agent → Fine-Tuned Model → Tool Calling → CRM / APIs / Internal Systems → Slack.

Example: "Check the accounts that haven't received a response in the last seven days and create follow-up tasks for the sales team." A chatbot explains how. An agent does it.

FINE-TUNING VS PROMPT ENGINEERING

A prompt tells the model what to do. Fine-tuning trains it on how your organization actually performs the task. That matters across thousands of repeated interactions. Retrieval, tool calling, structured prompts, and software logic often come first. Fine-tune when the model itself must specialize.

YOUR AI SHOULD UNDERSTAND YOUR BUSINESS

Combine company knowledge + fine-tuned behavior + tool calling + business APIs + human approval. That is the difference between having access to AI and building your own AI infrastructure.

FAQ

Q: What is Meta's Muse Glimmer?
A: A 30-billion-parameter open-weight agentic model for reasoning, coding, and administrative tasks. You can download, customize, and deploy it.

Q: Why fine-tune instead of using a closed AI API?
A: Closed models limit customization, data control, and infrastructure. Open-weight models let you adapt the AI to your workflows.

Q: Fine-tuning vs prompt engineering?
A: A prompt tells the model what to do. Fine-tuning trains it on how your organization actually does the work.

Q: Should every company fine-tune a model for Slack?
A: No. Retrieval, tool calling, structured prompts, and software logic often come first. Fine-tune when the model itself must become specialized.
