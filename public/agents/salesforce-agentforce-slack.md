# Salesforce Agentforce in Slack: AI Agents That Work Alongside Your Team

## Quick Answer

**Salesforce Agentforce** is a platform that turns AI agents into Slack teammates. You `@mention` them in channels and DMs just like any coworker. They access conversational context from Slack and connected business systems to answer questions, automate tasks, and execute actions — all within **Einstein Trust Layer** security guardrails. Choose from pre-built templates for HR, IT, Sales, Service, Marketing, Legal, Engineering, or Supply Chain, or build custom AI assistants using the Slack API.

## What Is Salesforce Agentforce?

Agentforce is Salesforce's platform for creating, deploying, and managing AI agents that operate alongside human employees. In Slack, these agents become active team members — they live in channels, participate in threads, respond to DMs, and take action autonomously within defined boundaries.

Unlike traditional chatbots, Agentforce agents use large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and real-time conversational data to understand context, reason through problems, and execute multi-step workflows.

## The Three Types of AI Agents in Slack

1. **Agentforce (Salesforce)** — Pre-built agent templates with enterprise-grade security. Best for teams that want ready-to-deploy solutions.
2. **Custom AI Assistants (Slack API)** — Build your own with your preferred LLM. Full control over behavior, data sources, and integration logic.
3. **Third-Party AI Assistants** — Pre-configured apps from Slack Marketplace (Adobe Express, Cohere, etc.).

## Conversational Context

Agentforce uses RAG on Slack's conversational data plus enterprise search to give agents relevant context. They search messages, files, and connected systems — delivering up to **40% improvement** in task performance compared to database-only agents.

## Slack Actions

Agentforce agents can: create channels, update canvases, send DMs, manage lists, trigger workflows, and track tasks — all autonomously within defined guardrails.

## Security: Einstein Trust Layer

- No customer data used for LLM training
- Data masking of sensitive information
- Continuous toxicity monitoring on outputs
- No-data-retention agreements with LLM providers
- Administrator-controlled data access scopes

## FAQ

**What is Salesforce Agentforce?**  
Salesforce's platform for AI agents that work as Slack teammates with autonomous action capabilities.

**How is Agentforce different from Slack's built-in AI?**  
Slack AI helps humans find information. Agentforce takes autonomous action — creating channels, updating canvases, executing workflows.

**Can agents access private channels?**  
Only if explicitly authorized. Administrators control all data access scopes.

**Does Agentforce use my data for training?**  
No. Salesforce never uses customer data to train LLMs.

**Can I build custom agents alongside Agentforce?**  
Yes. Slack supports Agentforce, custom API-built assistants, and third-party Marketplace apps.
